Invoice register · persisted in this browser

Statuses follow the model the MOF programme describes: the seller-side ASP validates and forwards, the buyer-side ASP responds with a Message Level Status (MLS), and Tax Data Documents are reported to the FTA. All of it is simulated here.

What happens after acceptance (concept)

The thesis: e-invoicing compliance is not a portal visit, it is the first step of the receivable's life cycle. Accepted invoices hand off cleanly into the workflow Volopay AR already describes: prepared collections, remittance matching and ERP update.
Contract / CRM deal→ Invoice drafted→ Compliance pre-validation (this product)→ Simulated ASP exchange · MLS→ Accepted invoice enters AR worklist→ Remittance → cash application → ERP updated
In this prototype the last row is represented conceptually in the register. Production mapping: see the architecture document.

Implemented here (prototype)

Browser UI (this page)→ Validation engine (JS rules cloned from MOF mandatory-fields v1.1)→ Persistence: browser storage (localStorage)→ Simulated ASP adapter (deterministic scenarios)
No network calls are made by the app. No real ASP, IRP or FTA system is touched. All sample buyers/sellers are fictional.

Proposed production architecture (not built)

ERP (NetSuite/Xero/QB/Tally)↔ Volopay AR services (contracts, billing readiness, collections, cash application)↔ Compliance-locked invoicing service (this product)→ Accredited ASP partner (UAE) / GSP + IRP (India)→ Buyer-side ASP · FTA reporting (TDD) · EmaraTax
Failure modes in production: ASP timeout (queue + retry with backoff), negative MLS (field-level diagnosis surfaces on the invoice), TDD report failure (hold invoicing state, alert), ERP sync conflict (write-back reconciliation). Security: org-level least-privilege API credentials per ASP, immutable audit log of submissions, PII minimisation (buyer endpoint IDs only).
